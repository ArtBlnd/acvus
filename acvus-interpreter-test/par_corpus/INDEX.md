# Parallel-loop corpus

Loops a sound compiler could run apart and the compiler at 5a01a67e did not, each with its ideal shape, its current shape from `acvus mir`, and the gap between them; plus negative controls that must stay sequential. A row whose gap class is `reached` prints its ideal's stages, cycles, orders and laws with the current binary. `facts/<id>.facts` holds what `acvus mir` prints of each case's `For`s, its cost line against the interpreter's table (RFC-0066 rule 8) among them, and `acvus-cli`'s `par_corpus` test runs every case against its expected value and those facts.

Every case is one `.acvus` in this directory. Its header states `id`, `family`, `desc` and `expect` (the run's stdout, `\n` between lines); a case that reads contexts states `contexts` and takes its inits from `inits/<id>/`, run in a space holding that script and those inits (`acvus ctl space add-script`, `acvus ctl space init`, `acvus run <name> --space <space>`). Every other case runs as `acvus run <file>`.

The current shape column pastes each `For`'s `stages [...]` line from `acvus mir <file>` and reads what each stage holds from `analysis::loop_deps` over the same pipeline (`printer::dump_with_facts`: `free`, or a cycle's token, order, law and operations; `‖` separates loops). For the context cases the reading is taken from the `acvus mir` listing itself. The ideal shape names stages S1, S2, ... in body order.

### The ten to unlock first

1. **T01** (reached, with T02, T04, T07, E02, E03, E06, E09, E10, X05 and E01's inner loop: the spawn and the eval run in the free stage and the Order cycle is `{merge}`) — The spawn and the `let` slot it borrows sit in the Order join although they read no token, so every `anyorder` IO loop issues its calls one chunk at a time. Moving work the cycle does not read out of the join is RFC-0089 rule 6's own statement; control is upfront, so the effects may run in the free stage (rule 5). The fix is in the cutter, and it frees T02, T04, T07, E02, E03, E06, E09, E10, X05.
2. **R01** (reached, with R12, R13, X07, O01, F07, T03 and E04; R20 and K10 hold two tokens in one cycle and stay) — A counter or sum under `if` (`if p { c = c + 1 }`) is the most common reduction in real loops; the diamond whose arms send `c + 1` and `c` is a merge on `+` with the identity on the skip arm. One reading in `analysis::loop_deps` frees R12, R13, R20, K10, X07, O01, F07, and the reduction half of C04/C10.
3. **S05** — Every search loop is one stage today because the pure predicate shares the exit's stage; RFC-0089 rule 5 already lets pure work run ahead and be discarded. Cutting before the exit branch frees S01-S04, S07, S08, S12, M09, and here the predicate (a regex) is the whole cost.
4. **W03** (reached, with W02, W04, W07, W11, W16, W14, N04, N07, N10, X08 and O05, by RFC-0089 rule 4's affine component, and W10 by `swap`'s `reaches(c[i], c[j])` (RFC-0082 rule 7); W05's `n - 1 - i` subtracts the counter from `n - 1`, which the body computes and RFC-0066 rule 3 therefore does not make invariant, and stays) — `out[i] = f(xs[i])` is the canonical map into a preallocated buffer; a store at the counter is RFC-0089's first-tier Open item and needs only the affine analysis that already exists (RFC-0066 rule 4). It frees W02-W05, W07, W11, W16, N10, X08, O05 and the outer loops of N04/N07.
5. **R04** (reached, with R05, R18, X04 and M05's `hi`, through `LawOp::Min`/`LawOp::Max`) — `if x < m { m = x }` is how min and max are written without the extern; recognizing the select as `min`/`max` (whose laws are declared) frees R05, R18, X04 and makes the extern's law reachable from ordinary code.
6. **M05** (reached, with O04: the law is read through the storage, which stays the token) — A sum or max read by reference after the loop (`s.to_string()`) becomes a Storage slot and loses its `+` law; the loan starts after the exit, so promoting the slot inside the loop is sound. It is the difference between O04 and a plain dot product, and it hides silently behind any later borrow.
7. **L05** — `while i < v.len()` is the most common counted loop RFC-0081 declines; with `v` unwritten in the body the bound is invariant. Converting it makes M10 and L05 ordinary `for`s and puts them in reach of everything above.
8. **N02** — A total accumulated in an inner loop (`total = total + x` inside `for x in &row`) leaves the outer loop `in_order`; a law through a nested loop is RFC-0089's first-tier Open item and covers N06, N09 and the outer cycle of E01.
9. **R08** (reached, with N08's inner loop and X02, as `LawOp::Concat` in order) — String concatenation is associative with identity `""`: declaring it as an ordered law (InOrder, combine by concat) frees R08, N08, X02, L12's body and is the base of R15 and C07.
10. **C01** — Prefix sums are the canonical scan; with the reduction already cut into its own stage, a scan law (RFC-0089 Open) frees C01, C02, C04-C06, C09, C10, P01 and is the base for C08/P05.
